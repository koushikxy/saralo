# Feature research

What the best apps in each area do, what Saralo already had, and what was added. Sources: Zapier and Wirecutter to-do reviews, Medisafe and GoodRx, WaterMinder and Healthline, Hevy vs Strong comparison (Oct 2026), Indyx wardrobe ranking, Bitwarden, AnyList, MoneyView's 2026 expense app list, Spendee.

| Area | Best apps | What they do well | Saralo before | Added |
|-|-|-|-|-|
| To-dos | Todoist, TickTick | Type "pay rent tomorrow 9am" and the date and time are picked up | Separate date and time pickers | Quick add that understands today, tomorrow, weekdays and times |
| Shopping | AnyList, Bring | Items sorted by aisle, recently used items one tap away | One flat list, long-press to delete | Groups (vegetables, dairy, staples...), recent items to re-add, delete button |
| Money | Spendee, Money Manager, axio | Budget alerts, monthly reports you can flip through | Current month only | Alerts at 80% and 100% of budget, month switcher |
| Bills | axio, CRED | Reminder before the due date | Reminder on the day | Extra reminder the day before |
| Medicines | Medisafe, MyTherapy | Pills-left count and refill reminders, adherence history | Times and taken ticks | Pills left, refill warning, last 7 days of doses |
| Water | WaterMinder, Plant Nanny | Reminders through the day, cup sizes, history | +/- 250 ml | Glass, mug, bottle buttons, reminders every 2 hours, 7-day history |
| Steps | Google Fit | Counts all day, weekly chart | Today only | Background counting (APK), 7-day chart |
| Gym | Hevy, Strong | Tick a set, rest timer starts, personal bests flagged | Sets and last-time hint | Set ticks, rest timer, new best badge |
| Passwords | Bitwarden | Password generator, weak and reused warnings | Save and copy | Generator, weak and reused badges, search |
| Documents | DigiLocker | Front and back, expiry alerts | Front photo, expiry date | Back photo, expiry badge and reminders 30 days before |
| Notes | Google Keep | Pin important notes | Pin existed in data only | Pin button |

Left out on purpose: SMS reading for automatic expenses (Play Store restricts SMS permission), cloud sync and sharing (Saralo has no server), AI outfit ideas (needs a server).
