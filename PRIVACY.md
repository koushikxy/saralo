# Saralo privacy policy

Saralo has no account and no server. Everything you add stays on your phone, encrypted. We cannot see it, and we do not sell or share anything.

## What is stored

Your planner, money entries, health logs, closet, vault items and settings. They are saved only on your device.

## Services the app talks to

- Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com): your approximate location or the city you type, to get the weather forecast.
- ipapi.co: only if you do not allow location, to guess your city from your internet address.

Nothing else leaves your phone.

## Permissions

- Camera and photos: to add pictures of bills, documents and clothes.
- Location: to show the weather for outfit ideas.
- Motion and fitness: to count steps.
- Notifications: to remind you about to-dos, events and reminders.
- Face ID or fingerprint: to unlock the private vault.

Each one is asked only when you use that feature, and you can say no.

## Backups

Backups are files you create yourself, locked with your password. You choose where to keep them. Without the password nobody can open them.

## Deleting your data

Settings, then Delete all my data, removes everything from your phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.

## Children

Saralo is not made for children under 13.
